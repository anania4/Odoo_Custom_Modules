Fleet Operations Pro

Comprehensive Fleet Management Solution for Odoo 18

Core Features

Vehicle & Asset Management

Comprehensive vehicle master data management with categories, status tracking, and detailed asset information. Keep track of all vehicle specifications, documents, and compliance requirements in one place.

Driver Management

Track driver information, licenses, and vehicle assignments. Set up automated alerts for license expirations to ensure compliance and safety.

Maintenance Management

Schedule and track all maintenance activities with service history and preventive maintenance scheduling. Reduce downtime with automated service reminders and maintenance forecasting.

Fuel Management

Track fuel consumption, fuel costs, and efficiency. Monitor fuel usage patterns and identify opportunities for efficiency improvements.

Compliance & Safety

Track certificates, insurance policies, and compliance documents with automated expiry alerts. Ensure your fleet meets all regulatory requirements.

Trip & Route Management

Plan and track trips with routes, distances, and toll integration for better operational efficiency.

Toll Management

Record toll locations and usage per vehicle, linked to trips, for complete route and toll visibility.

Analytics & Reporting

Vehicle analysis reports on fuel consumption, fuel costs, and trip utilization. Make data-driven decisions with per-vehicle fuel metrics.

Technical Information

Installation

  1. Copy the fleet_operations_pro folder to your Odoo addons directory
  2. Update the module list in Odoo
  3. Install the module from the Apps menu
  4. Configure your fleet settings and start managing your vehicles
